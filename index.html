01About

One man.
Two arenas.

In Kuwait most people know me as Coach Dehrab. Before I was a coach I was a kid who simply could not stay away from sport — a diver at four, a judoka at eleven, a fighter at eighteen.

Today I split my days between two things I take equally seriously: teaching people to control their own bodyweight, and building brands as a marketer. Different arenas, same rule — nothing can be rushed. Every skill is built one progression at a time.
